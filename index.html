05 / Answers

The questions people ask first.

How many changes do I get?

Changes are part of the process. We agree the number of rounds with you before work starts, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project. We give you a date before we begin, and we tell you early if anything moves.

Do I own the files?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the final files are yours.

What does it cost?

Every project is priced to its size. Tell us what you need and we send a clear quote before any work starts.
